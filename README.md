# 🌍 Travel Companion

> **Your Companion. Everywhere.**

Travel Companion is an **AI-powered personal travel assistant** designed to help travelers confidently explore any destination without depending on an unknown guide or multiple travel apps.

## ✨ What It Does

- 🧠 **AI Travel Companion** – Ask anything about your journey.
- 🗺️ **Smart Trip Planning** – Generate personalized day-by-day itineraries.
- 🗣️ **Language Translation** – Text, voice, signs, and menus.
- 🍜 **Food Assistant** – Discover local food and understand ingredients.
- 🚕 **Transport Guidance** – Find and understand local transportation.
- 💰 **Budget & Expense Tracking** – Manage spending and currency conversion.
- 📷 **AI Camera** – Understand signs, menus, landmarks, and documents.
- 🌦️ **Smart Recommendations** – Adapt plans based on weather, time, and location.
- 🆘 **Emergency Assistance** – Help users find appropriate official emergency services.
- 📚 **Travel Memory** – Remember trip details and preferences.

## 🎯 Example

Going to **Vietnam** without knowing Vietnamese?

Travel Companion can help you:

**Plan → Navigate → Translate → Eat → Travel → Explore → Stay Safe**

All through one AI companion.

## 🚀 Vision

To create a **global AI travel companion** that stays with travelers throughout their journey—from planning the trip to returning home.
